# Template: Status Update

Format for `status-rollup` and `stakeholder-digest`.

---

**Period:** | **Audience:** | **Overall:** on track / at risk / off track

## Headline
One sentence. If the reader stops here, what must they know.

## Progress since last update
What actually completed — outcomes, not activity. "Shipped X" beats "worked on X."

## At risk
What is slipping, why, and what you're doing about it. Anything that has slipped more
than once gets named explicitly — repeated slippage is a different problem from a single
delay and needs saying out loud.

## Decisions needed
What you need from this audience, from whom, and by when. Empty is a valid answer; vague
is not.

## Next period
The two or three things that will actually happen.

---

## Rules

- **Lead with the headline.** Executives read the first line and skim the rest.
- **Tailor the altitude, not the facts.** Different audiences get different detail, never
  different stories.
- **Bad news travels first.** Surfacing risk early is the entire value of a status update.
- **Always drafts.** Never posted or sent without explicit approval.
