# Glossary

Internal shorthand. Unexplained acronyms are the single largest source of bad agent
assumptions — a wrong expansion propagates into every downstream answer.

## Rules

- **Never guess an expansion.** Leave it as a TODO and ask.
- **Include what the thing *is*, not just what the letters stand for.** An expansion
  without a description is nearly useless to an agent.
- **Cover more than acronyms:** internal product codenames, team nicknames, phase names,
  maturity levels, and any term that means something different here than in the industry.

## Seeding this quickly

Skim two sprints of ticket titles and one recent status update. Anything you'd have to
explain to a new hire in their first week belongs here. Expect thirty to fifty entries
in most organizations; it takes about half an hour and is the highest-leverage file in
this folder.
