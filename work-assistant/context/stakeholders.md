# Stakeholders

Who owns, who decides, who needs informing. Ownership is the fact most often needed and
least often written down anywhere.

`last_verified: TODO`

## Template

```
## <Name>

- **Role:** 
- **Relationship:** manager | skip-level | peer | eng partner | design partner |
  stakeholder | exec sponsor | vendor
- **Owns / decides:** what genuinely requires their sign-off
- **Cares about:** the two or three things they ask about every time
- **Involved in:** [[initiative]] links
- **Update cadence:** how often they expect to hear from you, and through which channel
- **Working style:** detail level, format preference, how they like disagreement raised
- **last_verified:** YYYY-MM-DD
```

## Fill these first

1. **Your manager and skip-level** — cadence and what they care about
2. **Anyone with sign-off authority** on active initiatives
3. **Recurring meeting attendees** — `meeting-prep` depends on this
4. **Whoever you're currently blocked on**

## Notes

- **"Cares about" beats a title.** Knowing someone is a Director tells an agent nothing;
  knowing they always ask about timeline risk before scope shapes every draft.
- **Update cadence powers `stakeholder-digest`** — without it, that skill can't tell who
  has gone quiet.
- Keep it professional and factual. This file lives on a work machine and may be read by
  tooling with an audit trail.
