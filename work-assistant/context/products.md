# Products & Programs

The top-level things owned or contributed to. One section each.

## Template

```
## <Name>

- **What it is:** one plain sentence, no internal jargon
- **Who it serves:** the actual user or customer
- **Why it matters:** the business outcome it drives
- **Stage:** discovery | build | launch | scale | maintain | sunsetting
- **My role:** owner | contributor | consulted | informed
- **Key stakeholders:** [[stakeholder]] links
- **Systems involved:** [[system]] links
- **Active initiatives:** [[initiative]] links
- **Success measures:** how anyone knows this is working
- **last_verified:** YYYY-MM-DD
```

## Notes

- **"Why it matters" is the field agents need most** and the one most often left blank.
  Without it, an assistant can prioritize by due date but never by consequence.
- Keep entries at the level a skip-level would recognize. If a name means nothing outside
  your immediate team, it's probably an initiative, not a product.
